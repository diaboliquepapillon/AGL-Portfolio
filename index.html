Market Literacy

The AEMO concepts and frameworks that underpin every number in this analysis

⚡

Dispatch vs Pre-dispatch

The NEM runs a 5-minute dispatch cycle. AEMO's dispatch engine (NEMDE) clears the market every 5 minutes by matching generator offers against demand forecasts. Pre-dispatch provides 30-minute ahead price projections: critical for generators hedging intraday positions. The prices in this analysis are settlement prices derived from that dispatch process.

🔗

Interconnector Flows

Australia's five NEM regions (QLD, NSW, VIC, SA, TAS) are linked by high-voltage interconnectors. Power flows when price differentials exceed transmission losses. Direction is defined by convention: positive = exports from the source region. During SA price spikes, imports from VIC via the Heywood interconnector are a key moderating mechanism.

💲

Volume-Weighted Average Price

The price metric used here is the VWAP: each dispatch interval's price weighted by the volume of demand at that interval. The NEM's Market Price Cap is $16,600/MWh; the floor is −$1,000/MWh. Negative prices occur when renewables oversupply and generators pay to stay dispatched rather than reduce output.

📊

Operational Demand & the Duck Curve

NEM operational demand is met by scheduled and semi-scheduled generators: it excludes behind-the-meter rooftop solar. As rooftop penetration has grown, operational demand has flattened even as consumption rises. The result is the "duck curve": low midday demand followed by a steep evening ramp that gas peakers and batteries increasingly fill.

🌿

Renewable Proportion

Open Electricity counts scheduled, semi-scheduled, and non-scheduled renewable output (wind, solar, hydro, bioenergy) as a share of total operational demand. Curtailment: generation withheld due to grid constraints or negative prices: is reported separately and excluded from generation totals shown in this analysis.

🏭

Emissions Methodology

Emissions volumes are calculated using National Greenhouse Account (NGA) factors per fuel technology. The resulting intensity (kgCO₂e/MWh) is generation-weighted. This covers combustion emissions only: not lifecycle or embodied emissions. It is the methodology used in AEMO's Integrated System Plan modelling.
